## Galactic Chess Invaders Privacy Policy

Galactic Chess Invaders collects no personal data. The game runs entirely on
your Mac. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no
third-party components. It makes no network connections of any kind — the app is
sandboxed with no network access, so it is not capable of transmitting anything.

Your high score and your settings are saved on your own computer and are never
sent anywhere. Removing the app removes them.

You may optionally share crash diagnostics with Apple, but I do not receive any
personally identifiable information from those.

Galactic Chess Invaders does not collect data from anyone, including children
under 13.
